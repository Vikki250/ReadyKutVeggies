A few good habits

Keep it fresh.
Keep it simple.

Follow the package instructions first. These general tips can help you handle ready-cut vegetables carefully at home.

01

Refrigerate promptly

Keep fresh-cut vegetables chilled and return them to the refrigerator after use. Follow any storage instructions provided with your pack.

↗
02

Use clean hands and tools

Handle produce with clean hands and utensils. Keep it covered and separate from raw meat, poultry and seafood.

↗
03

Freeze with care

Where suitable, transfer vegetables to a freezer-safe, well-sealed container, label it with the date and follow food-safety guidance for the vegetable.

↗
04

Cook your way

Add to soups, stews, roasts, sautés or meal-prep dishes as appropriate. Cooking times vary with the vegetable and cut size.
